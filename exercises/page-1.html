A Tale of Two Cities

A novel by Charles Dickens, published in 1859

It was the best of times, it was the worst of times, it was the age of wisdom, it was the age of foolishness, it was the epoch of belief, it was the epoch of incredulity, it was the season of Light, it was the season of Darkness, it was the spring of hope, it was the winter of despair.

We had everything before us, we had nothing before us, we were all going direct to Heaven, we were all going direct the other way.

About the Novel

A Tale of Two Cities is a historical novel written by Charles Dickens and published in 1859. The story takes place in London and Paris before and during the French Revolution.

The novel explores themes of sacrifice, justice, love, and resurrection. It follows several characters whose lives become connected during a period of political and social change.

The Opening Lines

The famous opening contrasts opposite experiences and emotions. By describing the time as both the best and worst, Dickens introduces a world full of conflict, uncertainty, and change.

Charles Dickens

Charles Dickens was an English novelist known for writing about society, poverty, and the lives of ordinary people. His works remain widely read today.
